## Appendix C — Alternative: copyright assignment (stricter, use only if you want it)

Some projects ask contributors to **assign copyright** to the maintainer instead of licensing it.
Assignment gives the strongest relicensing and enforcement power and is the mechanism behind
projects that later re-licensed successfully. It is also noticeably harder to get contributors to
sign, and it removes rights from contributors permanently.

If You prefer assignment over Section 1, replace Section 1 with the following and delete Section 1:

```
1. Copyright Assignment

You hereby irrevocably assign to Feng Tao (Nate), GitHub user Fengtao1314520, all right, title and
interest, including copyright, in each of Your Contributions, together with the right to sue for
past infringement of the assigned rights. Feng Tao grants You back a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, royalty-free license to continue reproducing, modifying and distributing Your
Contribution for any purpose, including commercial purposes, and the right to sub-license it.
Feng Tao commits that Contributions assigned under this clause will continue to be published under
an open source or source available license for each version already released.
```

> ⚠️ Note on assignment in mainland China: copyright of a work created by a natural person can be
> transferred, but employer-created works and moral rights limit what can actually be transferred.
> For most independent projects the license grant in Section 1 already achieves the same practical
> result, so assignment is usually not worth the contributor friction.
